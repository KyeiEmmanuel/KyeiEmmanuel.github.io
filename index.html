01 · ABOUT

One mindset.
Different expressions.

I’m Nwafor Kelechi Stanley, creator behind Rickylove Art and Rickylove FX—building at the intersection of fine art, financial engineering, and quantitative system architecture.

My creative foundation began in visual artistry and editorial direction, where I developed a meticulous appreciation for observation, proportion, composition, and detail.

These same principles translated into financial markets, driving my work in price action dynamics, liquidity regimes, algorithmic risk engines, and technical scalping strategies (XAUUSD & BTCUSD).

My objective is to construct robust quantitative trading frameworks, MQL5 Expert Advisors, and research models grounded in statistical edge and capital protection.

“Observe carefully. Understand the structure. Develop a clear process. Improve consistently.”

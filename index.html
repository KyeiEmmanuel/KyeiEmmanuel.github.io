01 / The Person Behind the Work

Emmanuel Kyei.

One identity, expressed through different disciplines — art, markets and the architecture of ideas.

I’m Emmanuel Kyei, professionally known as Rickylove. My work is built around a simple principle: pay attention to what others overlook.

My foundation is visual art. Through graphite portraiture and realistic drawing, I learned to observe proportion, light, texture and detail. That discipline of looking closely became a way of thinking — and eventually extended into financial markets and quantitative research.

Today, I explore quantitative finance, systematic trading and system architecture alongside my creative practice. I’m interested in turning observations into clear frameworks, testing ideas carefully, and building systems that can be understood, measured and improved.

— Rickylove

03 / Quantitative Finance

Research & Systems.

My analytical work is focused on understanding markets, testing ideas and developing systematic frameworks — not selling predictions.

01

Market Research

Price action, market structure, liquidity, volatility and regime behaviour across financial markets.

02

Quantitative Research

Probability, statistics, backtesting, robustness analysis and the search for measurable market edges.

03

System Architecture

Rule-based trading logic, risk engines, automated execution and the design of systems that separate signal from risk.

Research first. Test the idea. Measure the result. Refine the process.

This is an evolving field of practice. The goal is not to appear finished — it is to build a stronger body of work through disciplined research.

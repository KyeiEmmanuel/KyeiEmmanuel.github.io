01 · ABOUT

One mindset.
Different expressions.

I’m Emmanuel Kyei, professionally known as Rickylove—a multidisciplinary creator building a personal brand at the intersection of art, financial markets, and structured thinking.

My creative journey began with Rickylove Art, where I developed a deep appreciation for observation, precision, composition, and detail.

These same principles naturally led me to financial markets, where I explore price action, market structure, liquidity, risk management, and quantitative research through Rickylove FX.

My long-term goal is to deepen my expertise in quantitative finance and build robust, systematic trading frameworks grounded in research, testing, probability, and disciplined risk management.

“Observe carefully. Understand the structure. Develop a clear process. Improve consistently.”

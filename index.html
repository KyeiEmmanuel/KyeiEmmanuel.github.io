01 · The person behind the work

A quiet eye.
Structured mind.

The work moves between visual art and analytical systems, connected by the same habit: study the structure before making the decision.

Observation became the foundation. Art developed the eye for proportion, detail and composition; financial research developed the discipline to test ideas, measure uncertainty and build repeatable processes.

Under the name Rickylove, creative work and market work live side by side. The aim is not to force them into one category, but to let each sharpen the other.

04 · Quantitative Finance & Systems

Research the idea.
Engineer the process.

A visual window into the analytical side: market structure, quantitative research, risk and system architecture. The graphics below are conceptual — not claimed performance results.
Markets are not a story.
They are a system.
The practice focuses on turning observations into measurable hypotheses, testing them against data, separating signal from noise and designing systems that remain understandable under pressure. Rickylove FX is the market-facing expression of that work.

05 · Working principles

Different tools.
One discipline.

The portfolio is intentionally broad, but the underlying method stays consistent: observe, structure, test, refine.
01

Observation

Look closely before deciding what something means — in a face, a room, a chart or a dataset.

02

Structure

Find the relationships that make the visible result possible, then make them clear.

03

Process

Turn intuition into a repeatable workflow that can be examined, improved and communicated.

04

Refinement

Remove noise, preserve what works and keep improving the quality of the final system.

01 · Profile

A visual eye.
A structured mind.

A personal practice built around observation, precision and disciplined systems thinking — expressed through art, spatial projects and quantitative research.

My work sits at the intersection of visual creativity and systems thinking.

Through art, I developed an eye for proportion, detail, composition and visual balance. Through quantitative finance, I developed a different discipline — researching ideas, measuring uncertainty and building systems that can be tested and refined.

Rickylove is the creative identity through which these disciplines come together. The work is intentionally diverse, but the underlying standard remains the same: observe carefully, structure clearly and refine relentlessly.

04 · Quantitative Finance & Systems

Research the idea.
Engineer the process.

Quantitative market research, systematic trading research and automated execution architecture across XAU/USD and digital assets.
Markets are not a story.
They are a system.
The research process turns market observations into quantitative hypotheses, tests them against historical data and evaluates robustness before translating validated ideas into systematic execution.

The focus is not simply finding entries. It is designing a complete process around research, risk, execution, validation and capital protection.

05 · Working Principles

Different tools.
One discipline.

Art, spatial design and quantitative research may use different tools, but the underlying approach remains consistent.
01

Observation

Look closely before deciding what something means — in a face, a room, a chart or a dataset.

02

Structure

Identify the relationships that create the visible result and make those relationships clear.

03

Process

Turn intuition into a repeatable workflow that can be examined, tested and improved.

04

Refinement

Remove noise, preserve what works and keep improving the quality of the final result.

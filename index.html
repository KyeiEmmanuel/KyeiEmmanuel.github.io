01 · ABOUT

A multidisciplinary practice with one mindset.

Emmanuel Kyei, professionally known as Rickylove, works across visual art, financial-market research and systematic thinking.

From the studio to the market.

My creative journey began through Rickylove Art, where drawing taught me the value of observation, proportion, composition and detail. Over time, those habits of looking closely naturally carried into financial markets.

Through Rickylove FX, I study price action, market structure, liquidity, volatility and risk. My interest in quantitative finance grows from the same desire: turning observation into a clearer framework for understanding complex systems.

This website brings those worlds together — not as separate identities, but as different expressions of the same approach to learning, creating and solving problems.
